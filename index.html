FAQ

Things people actually ask.

How long does a typical project take?

Brand identities ship in three weeks, websites in three to six, social packages run monthly. Every quote includes a written timeline before kickoff.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes — we currently work with teams in Uganda, Rwanda, the UK, and the UAE. Everything ships digitally or via DHL.

Can I order merch without a full branding project?

Absolutely. Add merchandise items to your cart and check out — you can either send us your existing artwork or commission us to create something new.

What payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa, bank transfer, and card payments. For corporate orders we issue a 14-day invoice. We confirm payment details with you directly on WhatsApp after you order.
